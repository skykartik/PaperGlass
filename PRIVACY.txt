PaperGlass - Privacy Policy and Terms
Version 2.0

PaperGlass makes your Windows display look like e-ink or paper. It is designed to work entirely on
your own computer.

1. What PaperGlass does not do
   - It does not connect to the internet. It has no accounts, no analytics, no telemetry and no ads.
   - It does not collect, upload or share any information about you or your computer.
   - It does not capture, record or read what is on your screen. It only changes how colours are
     displayed and draws a transparent layer (paper texture, lamp glow) on top.
   - It does not log your keystrokes. Global hotkeys are registered with Windows, which tells
     PaperGlass only when one of your chosen shortcuts is pressed.

2. What is stored on your computer
   - Your preferences (looks, sliders, schedule, shortcuts, sound options) are saved in
     %APPDATA%\PaperGlass\settings.json. Nothing else is saved.
   - If you use the app exceptions feature, the program names you add (for example vlc.exe) are saved
     in that same file.
   - To apply app exceptions, PaperGlass reads the name of the program that is in front. This happens
     only on your computer, is not stored, and is not sent anywhere.

3. Startup
   - PaperGlass starts with Windows only if you ask it to (in the installer or in Settings). This adds
     one entry named PaperGlass under your user's Windows startup list, which you can turn off at any
     time in Settings or in Windows Settings > Apps > Startup.

4. Removing PaperGlass
   - The uninstaller removes the program and its startup entry, and asks whether to delete your
     settings folder as well. Closing or removing PaperGlass immediately returns your display colours
     to normal.

5. Changes to this policy
   - If a future version changes how your information is handled, this document will be updated and
     shown again during installation.

Terms of use
   PaperGlass is provided "as is", without warranty of any kind. You use it at your own risk. It is
   not a medical product and does not treat or prevent any eye or health condition. Take regular
   breaks from screens.
